# R154 prereg — phase 7 pilot: per-slice Alloy structural models on confirmed slices (2026-10-26)

Owner ruling (2026-10-26-owner-rulings-r153-phase7-history.md):
phase 7 starts as a pilot, not the full build. The change-guard
plan's "not until phase 5 exits" gates goldenizing, not a
core-capability pilot.

## Question

Three slices already carry confirmed TLA+ verdicts. For each,
can a small Alloy model of the slice's *state structure* (sig
relations over one snapshot, operation predicates between two
snapshots — no temporal operators; ordering stays in TLA+) be
authored quickly, solved with the engine's `run_alloy`, and say
something the temporal verdict did not? The pilot measures
feasibility and added value, not new defects.

## Slices and structural properties

- kombu `Resource` (R152 confirmed limit race): snapshots relate
  `idle` queue contents, `dirty` (checked out), and `created`.
  Structural assert: idle and dirty are disjoint and no resource
  has two holders. Transition predicates model acquire's
  check-then-act steps so a two-step trace can test whether
  `|dirty| > limit` is structurally reachable.
- rich `Text` spans (R148/R150 confirmed span race): spans as
  `[start, end)` relations over the joined text. Structural
  assert: spans exactly tile the text (ordered, non-overlapping,
  covering) in every snapshot the append transitions produce.
- redis `BlockingConnectionPoolLocked` (R148 confirmed): lock
  snapshots relate holder, recursion count, and the `_locked`
  flag. Structural assert: `count > 0` iff a holder exists, and
  the flag is set only while some acquire is outstanding.

## Method

Models live benchmark-side under `/tmp/r154/` (codebases are
GPL/local-only; nothing committed but this prereg and the results
doc, which quotes outcomes without model text). Checked with the
engine's own `run_alloy` API, JDK via the portable wrapper.
Each model: one satisfiable witness `run`, one `check` of its
structural assert, small scopes (≤4, ≤3 steps where traced).

## Predictions

- P-R154-1: all three models solve; every witness run is
  satisfiable.
- P-R154-2: at least one structural assert fails with a
  counterexample instance that mirrors its slice's temporal
  defect (kombu limit, rich span tiling, or redis flag/holder).
- P-R154-3: authoring cost is small enough to scale: each model
  lands in under ~150 lines and one iteration of scope/idiom
  fixes; if a slice needs heroics, that is the pilot's answer
  for that slice shape and is reported as such.
- P-R154-4: no Alloy result contradicts its slice's TLA+ verdict.
  A contradiction stops the pilot for adjudication, not for
  silent preference of either checker.
